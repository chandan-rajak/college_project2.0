Admission Process

Welcome to Gaya College Of Engineering, Gaya. Complete your admission process smoothly with all important instructions, required documents, and fee details.

Admission Process

Step 1 - Registration

Complete online registration through UGEAC/BCECE portal.

Step 2 - Choice Filling

Select GCE Gaya and preferred branch carefully.

Step 3 - Seat Allotment

Download allotment letter after seat confirmation.

Step 4 - Verification

Bring all original documents for verification.

Step 5 - Fee Payment

Pay admission fee through online payment gateway.

Step 6 - Reporting

Report physically at GCE Gaya campus.
